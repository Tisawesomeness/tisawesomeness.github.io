The final 26.4 features, including yet another highly-requested block set and a secret block...

## Music Disc

The "Hyper Potions - flurry" music disc plays for 2:34, and can be found in Igloo basement chests with a 30% chance.

## Stray

Strays now spawn in the following biomes:

| Biome             | Pack Size | Weight | Chance  |
| ----------------- | --------- | ------ | ------- |
| Jagged Peaks      | 4         | 95/620 | 15.323% |
| Snowy Slopes      | 4         | 95/620 | 15.323% |
| Frozen Peaks      | 4         | 95/635 | 14.961% |
| Frozen River      | 4         | 95/641 | 14.937% |
| Deep Frozen Ocean | 4         | 95/640 | 14.844% |
| Frozen Ocean      | 4         | 95/640 | 14.844% |

## Misc

Players in spectator mode now spawn and respawn at their exact spawn or respawn point, even if that position is inside a block.

## Secret Block

<details markdown="block">
<summary>Secret Block</summary>

Added a new block not shown in the creative inventory: **Chiseled Soulshale** (`chiseled_soulshale`).

It can obtained in survival from non-icebox Ancient City chests, in the main loot pool with 5-10 rolls:

| Item               | Weight | Chance  |
| ------------------ | ------ | ------- |
| Chiseled Soulslate | 3/87   | 23.002% |

Chiseled Soulshale has **2 hardness** and **6 blast resistance**.

The best tool for Chiseled Soulshale is a pickaxe. It drops when mined by hand or with any tool.

Chiseled Soulshale shows up as {::colordef:#3A8E8C} "Warped Stem" on a map.

When absorbed by a Sulfur Cube, Chiseled Soulshale is part of the "Slow Bouncy" archetype.

</details>

## Data Packs

### Commands

The `/effect` command now applies or clears all mob effects in the provided tag, excluding effects the target mob is immune to. The command's result is the total number of effects applied or cleared.

### Data Components

Inventory item tooltips now support line breaks using the newline character `\n`.
