Several technical changes and bugfixes.

## Abandoned Camp

The world is split into **37×37** (up from 34×34) chunk squares, and an abandoned camp is placed at a random position within each square (unless generation fails). Abandoned camps are at least 8 chunks away from each other in the X and Z directions and at most **66** (up from 60) chunks away.

## Drowned

If a drowned's target goes out of range or exits the water, drowned will now become neutral again after 20-39 seconds.

## Projectiles

Arrows deflecting off of entities now deflect off of the margin drawn around the entity for collision purposes, not the entity's bounding box itself, fixing [MC-305214](https://bugs.mojang.com/browse/MC/issues/MC-305214).

Fireballs and wind charges shot with a projectile will still fly in the player's facing direction. The fix to [MC-101770](https://bugs.mojang.com/browse/MC/issues/MC-101770) was reverted before the snapshot released.

## Data Packs

### Entity Data

Drowned now store `angry_at` and `anger_end_time` like other neutral mobs.

### Features

The `desert_well` **feature type** was removed. The `desert_well` **feature** still exists and now references two new structure templates: `desert_well/well` and `desert_well/suspicious_sand`.
