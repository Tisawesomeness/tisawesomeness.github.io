A few more tweaks to Abandoned Camps and data packs.

## Data Packs

### Commands

`/recipe` no longer supports hardcoded recipes (type starts with `minecraft:crafting_special_`). These recipes will always be unlocked when `limited_crafting` is true.

### Entity Data

`invulnerable_time` is now used for the 3-second invulnerability given to skeletons and skeleton horses spawned from a skeleton trap, and for the 1-second invulnerability given the dropped cushion item after being struck by lightning. Invulnerability from these sources now persists after unloading and loading.
