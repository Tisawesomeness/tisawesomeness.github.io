A second pre-release with several bugfixes.

## Entities

Fluid source blocks underneath a block with a sturdy downwards face visually appear as a full block, but previously if an entity's eye position was in the top 1/9th of the block, that entity would not be considered underwater. This was fixed for all entities as part of fixing {::bug:MC-311186}.

## Blocks

Snow and ice melting into water now emits a `block_destroy` vibration (frequency 12) to fix {::bug:MC-210117}. Frosted ice partially melting does not emit a vibration.
