## Ice Crystal

Ice Crystals are 0.4375 × 0.625 blocks in size, have **1.5 hardness** and **1.5 blast resistance**, and emit a light level of 4.

The best tool for Ice Crystals is a pickaxe. Ice Crystals always drop when broken by hand, any tool, or a piston.

Ice Crystals show up as {::colordef:#A0A0FF} "Ice" on a map. When placed under a note block, they make the "Chime" instrument sound.

## Icicle

Icicles are the same size as other speleothem-like blocks, and have **0.5 hardness** and **0.5 blast resistance**.

Icicles always drop when broken by hand, any tool, or a piston.

Icicles show up as {::colordef:#6699D8} "Light Blue" on a map. When placed under a note block, they make the "Chime" instrument sound.

Icicles can only be placed vertically up or down, not in any direction. An icicle stalagmite and stalactite facing each other will not merge their tips together.

When an icicle stalactite is exposed to a block light level of 5 or greater, is 1 or 2 blocks above a {::hint:magma block:`#melts_icicle_above` block tag}, or is {::hint:in the Nether:`gameplay/water_evaporates` environment attribute}, it starts to melt:
- If the tip is random ticked, it falls if the block below the tip is air
- If the middle or frustum is random ticked, it falls
- If the base is random ticked, it does not fall, but it does not grow as usual

### AI

If a Frostbite is holding an Ice Ball, it alternates between ranged and melee attacks:
- It does ranged attacks until the player is less than 3 blocks away (though it will only throw Ice Balls if the player is within 16 blocks)
- Then, it does melee attacks until either the player is more than 5 blocks away or the Frostbite cannot pathfind to the player

### Melee Attack

Frostbites perform a melee attack once every 1.5 seconds instead of once every second like other zombies. A Frostbite melee attack inflicts Freezing for 3 seconds.

### Ranged Attack

Frostbites throw an Ice Ball once every 3 seconds on Easy, once every 1-2 seconds on Normal, and once every 0.75-1.5 seconds on Hard. Frostbites throw Ice Balls with the same power and uncertainty as Snow Golems throwing snowballs.

Unlike Snow Golems, Frostbites do not stop moving when throwing Ice Balls.

Villagers killed by an Ice Ball thrown by a Frostbite will zombify.

## Freezing

Added Potion of Freezing (0:45) and Potion of Freezing (1:30).

The Freezing effect causes entities (not just players) to start freezing as if they were in powder snow. Entities take 7 seconds to fully freeze, freezing twice as fast if they are also in powder snow. Once fully frozen, an entity takes 1.5 freezing damage (up from the 1 damage powder snow normally does) every 2 seconds (based on a two-second timer starting when the entity is created or loaded).

Freezing entities are gradually slowed and have their jump height reduced, just like if they were in powder snow. When an entity gains the Freezing effect, it does not slow down like an entity entering a powder snow block, however.

Players do not take any damage from Freezing if the `freeze_damage` gamerule is enabled. Mobs that are weak to freezing damage, such as Blazes, will take extra Freezing damage. Mobs with Freezing do **not** take extra damage from other sources.

Once an entity no longer has the Freezing effect and is no longer in powder snow, it takes 3.5 seconds for the entity to fully thaw, taking less time if the entity was not completely frozen.

Mobs wearing leather armor, regular Skeletons, and Sulfur Cubes with an absorbed block do not freeze. If a mob was freezing, then puts on leather armor, or a Sulfur Cube absorbs a block, it starts thawing.

Skeletons do **not** convert to Strays if they have the Freezing effect. Skeletons still must be inside a powder snow block.

## Ice Ball

Ice Balls stack to 16 items. Players and dispensers throw Ice Balls with the same power and uncertainty as snowballs.

Ice Balls are 0.25 × 0.25 blocks in size. They deal $$\lceil 1.395 \cdot \text{velocity} \rceil$$ damage.
